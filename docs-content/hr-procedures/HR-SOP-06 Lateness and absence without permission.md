# [HR-SOP-06] Lateness and absence without permission

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

What to do when someone is late, or doesn't turn up and hasn't told us, so that their safety comes first, the shift is covered, and it is handled fairly.

Applies to: duty managers, supervisors and line managers, at every LeisureWorld centre.

## When someone doesn't turn up

1. **Cover the shift first** (HR-SOP-02, Covering the shift). Pool safety comes before anything else.
2. **Within Set by LeisureWorld (for example, 30 minutes) of their start time**, call them. If there is no answer, leave a message and try again.
3. If you still can't reach them, call their emergency contact. Say only that they haven't arrived and we are checking they are all right.
4. If you are worried for their safety and can't reach anyone, tell the centre manager. They decide whether to ask the Gardaí to check on them.
5. Keep trying each day. If you haven't heard from them for **Set by LeisureWorld** working days, the line manager writes to them asking them to get in touch by a set date.

## When they get back in touch

1. Ask what happened. There may be a good reason (an accident, a family emergency, being too unwell to call).
2. **If the reason is acceptable**, record it as an absence with the right reason (HR-SOP-02) and remind them how to report an absence next time.
3. **If there is no acceptable reason**, record it in Turnfin with the reason **Other** and a short note "No permission, not reported". The line manager decides whether the disciplinary procedure applies.
4. Don't take money off their pay unless their contract allows it and the law permits it. Unpaid time not worked is normally simply not paid; check with **Set by LeisureWorld (payroll)**.

## Lateness

1. If someone is late, cover their place until they arrive. Note the time they arrived in the rota's change log if it changed the day.
2. On a first or occasional late arrival, have a quick word that day.
3. If lateness happens **Set by LeisureWorld** times in **Set by LeisureWorld** weeks, the line manager holds an informal talk, as in HR-SOP-04 Step 1, and records it on their HR record.
4. If it carries on, follow the formal stages in HR-SOP-04 or the disciplinary procedure.
5. Lateness that leaves a pool short of its minimum lifeguards is a safety matter: tell the centre manager the same day.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- The absence, in **Rota: Absences**.
- Cover and changes to the day, in the rota's change log.
- Talks and outcomes, as notes on their **HR** record.
