# [HR-SOP-09] Absence records and medical privacy

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

What we record about absence, where, who can see it, and how we keep medical information private, as data protection law requires.

Applies to: everyone who records or reads absence, sickness or leave, at every LeisureWorld centre.

## The rules

- Health information is special category personal data under GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We keep as little as we need, only for as long as we need it, and only the people who need it can see it.
- **Never write a diagnosis, symptoms, medication or other medical details in Turnfin**, in a rota note, an HR note, a task, an email or a group chat.
- Talk about someone's absence only with the people who need to know to cover their work or manage their attendance.

## Where each thing is kept

- **That someone is off, and the days**: on the Rota. Everyone who sees the rota sees only that they are off.
- **The reason** (sickness, family emergency, bereavement, other): in **Rota: Absences**. Only people who run the rota see it.
- **Return-to-work answers**: in **Rota: Absences**, shown on their HR record. Seen by people who run the rota and those who can read their HR record.
- **Talks, agreements and warnings**: notes on their **HR** record. Who sees them depends on the note: Only me, On their record, or Shared with them.
- **Medical certificates and self-certification forms**: **Set by LeisureWorld (for example, a locked file held by the operations manager or payroll)**. Never in Turnfin.
- **Occupational health reports**: kept by the operations manager only. Seen by the operations manager and the staff member.

## Steps for anyone recording absence

1. Record the absence in Rota with the right reason. Keep any note short and practical.
2. Hand paper certificates to **Set by LeisureWorld** the same week. Don't photograph them on your phone, scan them into Turnfin, or leave them at the desk.
3. Choose HR note visibility with care: **Only me** for your own working notes, **On their record** for things other managers need, **Shared with them** for anything agreed with the person.
4. If you see medical details written somewhere they shouldn't be, tell the operations manager so they can be removed.

## How long records are kept

**Set by LeisureWorld (retention period for absence and sickness records, and for certificates)**. Records are deleted or destroyed safely when no longer needed.

## Staff rights

Staff can ask to see the information we hold about them. Superadmins in Turnfin can export everything held about a person's employment for such a request. Pass every request to the operations manager straight away; there is a time limit to reply.

## If something goes wrong

If absence or medical information is sent to the wrong person, lost or seen by someone who shouldn't see it, tell the operations manager at once. It may need reporting to the Data Protection Commission within the time the law sets.
