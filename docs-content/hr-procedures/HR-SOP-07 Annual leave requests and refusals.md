# [HR-SOP-07] Annual leave requests and refusals

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

How staff ask for annual leave and how line managers approve or refuse it fairly, while keeping the centre safely staffed.

Applies to: every member of staff and their line manager, at every LeisureWorld centre.

## The rules

- Everyone builds up paid annual leave under the Organisation of Working Time Act, worked out on the hours they work. Their own entitlement is in their contract (**Set by LeisureWorld**).
- The company decides when leave is taken, after thinking about the person's family needs and their chance to rest, and the needs of the centre. It must be agreed in advance; it can't simply be taken.
- Leave is meant to be taken in the leave year. Carrying leave over is **Set by LeisureWorld (how much, and until when)**, and is allowed when the person couldn't take it because of illness.
- Bank holidays (public holidays) are separate from annual leave.

## How staff ask

1. Ask your line manager by **Set by LeisureWorld (form, email or Timepoint)** at least **Set by LeisureWorld (for example, four weeks)** before the first day.
2. Don't book travel until it is approved.

## How the line manager decides

1. Reply within **Set by LeisureWorld (for example, one week)**.
2. Check the rota for those days: open the **Plan** for the week and look at who else is off and whether minimum staffing (lifeguards, swim teachers, reception) can still be met.
3. Approve requests in the order they came in, unless there is a fair reason not to.
4. Busy periods with limits: **Set by LeisureWorld (for example, school holidays, summer, Christmas; how many people from one department can be off at once)**.
5. **If you approve**, record it where leave is kept (**Set by LeisureWorld**) and keep them off the rota for those days.
6. **If you have to refuse**, tell them in person first, give the reason, and offer other dates. Confirm in writing. Never refuse because of who they are (equality grounds), for example because they have children or are older.
7. If someone disagrees with a refusal, they can raise it with the centre manager.

## Cancelling approved leave

Only in an emergency, and only after speaking to the centre manager. If you do, talk to the person, explain why, and cover any costs they can't get back, as **Set by LeisureWorld** allows.

## Leaving employment

Leave not taken is paid when someone leaves; leave taken beyond what they had built up is **Set by LeisureWorld**.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- Approved leave keeps them off the **Rota** for those days. Annual leave is not recorded as an absence in Rota: Absences.
- Leave balances are kept in **Set by LeisureWorld (Timepoint or payroll)**.
- A refusal or a cancelled leave can be noted on their **HR** record.
