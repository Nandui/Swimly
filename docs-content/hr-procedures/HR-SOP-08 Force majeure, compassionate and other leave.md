# [HR-SOP-08] Force majeure, compassionate and other leave

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

How to handle urgent family leave, bereavement and the other kinds of leave the law gives, so that staff get what they are entitled to and it is recorded properly.

Applies to: line managers and the operations manager, at every LeisureWorld centre.

## Force majeure leave (urgent family reasons)

For when a close family member is suddenly injured or ill and the staff member's presence is urgently needed. It is paid and limited to a small number of days set by law.

1. The staff member tells the person on shift in charge as for any absence (HR-SOP-01), and says it is a family emergency.
2. Record it in **Rota: Absences** with the reason **Family emergency** and cover the shift (HR-SOP-02).
3. On their return, they fill in the force majeure leave form (**Set by LeisureWorld: where**) saying who was ill or injured and why they were needed. The line manager checks it qualifies; if not, it can be taken as annual leave or unpaid leave by agreement.
4. Force majeure leave never counts towards absence triggers (HR-SOP-04).

## Compassionate and bereavement leave

1. Be kind and flexible. Cover the shifts first; paperwork can wait.
2. Record it in **Rota: Absences** with the reason **Bereavement**.
3. How many paid days, and for which relatives, is **Set by LeisureWorld**. Extra time can be agreed as annual leave or unpaid leave.
4. Check in with them on their return, and mention the employee assistance programme (**Set by LeisureWorld**) if there is one.

## Other leave given by law

These are asked for in advance, with the notice the law sets. Pass every request to the operations manager, who confirms it in writing:

- Maternity, paternity, adoptive, parent's and parental leave.
- Carer's leave.
- Leave for medical care purposes (to care for a child or a person they live with who needs serious care).
- Domestic violence leave. Treat any request with care and privacy; the reason never goes on the rota or in a note.
- Jury service (paid, with the summons as proof).
- Time off for ante-natal classes and visits, and for breastfeeding.

Each has its own rules on notice, length and pay. The operations manager checks the current rules on the Citizens Information or Workplace Relations Commission website before confirming.

## Flexible and remote working requests

Parents and carers can ask for flexible working. The line manager passes the request to the operations manager, who must reply within the time the law sets and give reasons if refusing.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- Short-notice leave (family emergency, bereavement) in **Rota: Absences**, with the matching reason.
- Planned statutory leave keeps the person off the **Rota** for those days. A note of the dates can go on their **HR** record; never the private reason for domestic violence leave.
- Forms and letters go in their personnel file (**Set by LeisureWorld: where**).
