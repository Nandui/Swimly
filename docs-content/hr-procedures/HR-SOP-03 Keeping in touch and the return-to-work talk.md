# [HR-SOP-03] Keeping in touch and the return-to-work talk

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

How the line manager stays in touch with someone who is off sick, and holds a short return-to-work talk with everyone who comes back after any sickness absence.

Applies to: line managers, at every LeisureWorld centre. The talk happens after every sickness absence, even one day.

## Keeping in touch while they are off

1. **Off for a few days:** the staff member calls in each day (HR-SOP-01). You don't need to do more.
2. **Off for more than Set by LeisureWorld days, or open-ended:** agree with them how and when you will be in touch (for example a call every week). Write it as a short note on their HR record.
3. Each time you talk, ask how they are, whether there is anything we can do to help, and whether they know when they might be back. Keep it short and supportive. Don't pressure them to come back early.
4. Make sure their medical certificates arrive and are passed on (HR-SOP-02).
5. If they are off for **Set by LeisureWorld weeks**, or you think they might be off for a long time, follow HR-SOP-05.

## The return-to-work talk

Hold it on their first day back, or as soon as possible after. It takes five to ten minutes, in private.

1. In **Rota: Absences**, people who are due a return to work are listed. Open theirs and choose **Return to work**.
2. Talk it through:
   - Welcome them back. Ask how they are.
   - Check the dates they were off are right.
   - Ask, in general terms, whether they are fit for their normal duties. You don't need to know the diagnosis.
   - Ask whether anything at work caused or added to the absence (a strain, an incident, stress). If so, see Incidents and the operations manager.
   - Ask whether they need anything to help them settle back (lighter duties for a few days, a change of hours). Agree it if you can.
   - Tell them anything that changed while they were off.
   - If it was more than self-certified days, check the medical certificate has come in.
   - If this is one of several absences, mention it gently and say that the absence procedure (HR-SOP-04) may apply. Don't start a formal step in this talk.
3. In Turnfin, answer **Are they fit to work?** with **Fit to work** or **Back with changes**, answer **Has their fit note come in?** if it asks, and save. This closes the absence.
4. If you agreed changes, put them on the rota and add a short note on their HR record saying what was agreed and until when. No medical details.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- The return to work, its answer and the fit note question, in **Rota: Absences** (shown on their HR record).
- Keeping-in-touch arrangements and agreed changes as a note on their **HR** record, visibility **On their record** or **Shared with them**.
