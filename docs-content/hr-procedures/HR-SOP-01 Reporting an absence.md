# [HR-SOP-01] Reporting an absence

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

So that every member of staff knows how to tell us they can't come in, who to tell and by when, and so that the shift can be covered in time.

Applies to: every member of staff, at every LeisureWorld centre, including casual and part-time staff.

## What the staff member does

1. **Phone, don't text.** Call the person on shift in charge at your centre as early as you can, and no later than **Set by LeisureWorld (for example, two hours before your shift starts; for an early opening shift, by a set time the evening before or at opening)**.
2. Use the number for your centre: **Set by LeisureWorld (duty manager phone for each centre)**. If nobody answers, leave a message and call again until you speak to someone. A text, WhatsApp or social media message on its own is not enough. **Set by LeisureWorld (if a text is ever accepted, say when)**.
3. Say:
   - that you can't come in, and which shift or shifts;
   - the reason in general terms (for example "I'm sick", "a family emergency"). You don't have to give medical details;
   - when you expect to be back, if you know;
   - anything that needs handing over (keys, a class you were due to teach, a booking you were looking after).
4. **If you are still off the next day**, call again each day before your shift, unless your line manager has agreed a different way to keep in touch (HR-SOP-03).
5. **If you are off for more than Set by LeisureWorld days**, get a medical certificate from your doctor and send it to **Set by LeisureWorld (who and how)**. Statutory sick pay can only be paid for days covered by a medical certificate.
6. When you are ready to come back, tell your line manager the day before if you can, so you are on the rota.

## If you are going to be late

Call the person on shift in charge as soon as you know, before your start time, and say when you will arrive.

## If you become unwell at work

Tell the person on shift in charge before you leave. Don't just go. If you are in a ratio-critical role (lifeguard, swim teacher), wait until someone takes over unless it is an emergency.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

Nothing by the staff member. The person you speak to records it (HR-SOP-02).
