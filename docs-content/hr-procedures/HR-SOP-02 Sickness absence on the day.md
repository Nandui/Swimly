# [HR-SOP-02] Sickness absence on the day

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

What the person on shift in charge does when a member of staff calls in sick: take the call, cover the shift, record it in Turnfin and tell the line manager.

Applies to: duty managers and supervisors who hold Rota: Run, at every LeisureWorld centre.

## Taking the call

1. Be kind and brief. Ask:
   - which shifts they will miss;
   - the reason in general terms (sickness, family emergency, bereavement or other). Don't ask for medical details;
   - when they think they will be back;
   - anything you need to know to cover their work (a class, keys, a booking).
2. If they texted instead of phoning, call them back. Remind them of HR-SOP-01 next time you speak, not while they are unwell.
3. If they sound very unwell or in danger, ask if they need help and, if so, call 112 or 999.

## Covering the shift

1. Open **Rota** in Turnfin and go to **Today** (or the **Plan** for a later day).
2. Their places show under **Not covered yet**. Tap the gap and use **Choose someone**.
3. Pick someone who is free and qualified. Turnfin warns you if they are missing a certificate, would go over their hours, or would miss a break they are owed.
4. When it asks for a reason, choose **Covering an absence**.
5. If you can't cover a lifeguard or swim teacher place, follow the pool's normal operating procedure (close a lane or area, or cancel the class) and tell the centre manager. Never run a pool short of its minimum lifeguards.
6. Keep the change log up to date for Timepoint: mark it **Done in Timepoint** once it is entered there.

## Recording the absence

1. In **Rota**, open **Absences** and choose **Report an absence**.
2. Choose the person, the first day off, the reason, and the last day if you know it. Leave the last day empty if you don't.
3. If they were off recently, Turnfin asks **Is this an extension of that absence?** or **Is it the same thing again?** Answer it: it matters for HR-SOP-04.
4. Add a short note only if it helps the rota (for example "will call Thursday"). **Never write medical details** in the note.

## Telling the line manager

Let the person's line manager know the same day, by **Set by LeisureWorld (for example, a message or email)**, if it isn't you.

## If they are off again tomorrow

- If they ring in again, open their absence in **Absences** and choose **Extend**.
- If they don't ring in by the agreed time, call them. If you can't reach them, follow HR-SOP-06.

## Certification

- Up to **Set by LeisureWorld** days, the staff member fills in a self-certification form on their return.
- Beyond that, they need a medical certificate. Turnfin asks **Has their fit note come in?** at the return to work for sickness of more than seven days.
- Statutory sick pay is only paid for days covered by a medical certificate. Pass certificates to **Set by LeisureWorld (who keeps them)**; don't scan them into Turnfin.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- The absence (person, days, reason) in **Rota: Absences**. It shows on their HR record under Absences and returns to work.
- The cover, with the reason **Covering an absence**, in the rota's change log.
