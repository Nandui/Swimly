# [HR-SOP-05] Long-term sickness absence

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

How we support someone who is off sick for a long time, get medical advice through occupational health, and plan a safe return, often a phased one.

Applies to: line managers and the operations manager, at every LeisureWorld centre. Long-term means off for **Set by LeisureWorld (for example four weeks)** or more, or expected to be.

## Steps

1. **Tell the operations manager** when someone reaches the long-term point or is expected to. From here they guide the case with the line manager.
2. **Keep in touch** as agreed (HR-SOP-03), usually once a week or fortnight. Keep a note of each contact on their HR record. Ask how they are, not when they will be back.
3. **Keep certificates coming.** Each medical certificate should cover the whole time off. Chase gently if one runs out.
4. **Refer to occupational health** after **Set by LeisureWorld** weeks, or sooner if the doctor's advice is unclear:
   - Explain to the staff member why, and get their written consent before sending the referral.
   - Ask practical questions: when they might return, what duties they can and can't do, whether a phased return or changes would help, whether a disability may be involved.
   - The report comes to the operations manager. Share only what the line manager needs to plan work.
5. **Plan the return** with the staff member, using the occupational health advice:
   - **Phased return:** fewer hours or days at first, building up over **Set by LeisureWorld** weeks. Agree pay during the phased return (**Set by LeisureWorld**).
   - **Changes to duties:** for example no lifeguarding until they can do the rescue test, or no lifting. Check pool qualifications are still current (Training).
   - Write the plan down and give them a copy.
6. **On their return**, hold the return-to-work talk (HR-SOP-03) and choose **Back with changes** if there is a phased return or other changes. Put the agreed hours on the rota.
7. **Review** the plan every week or two until they are back to normal duties.

## If a return isn't possible

If medical advice says they can't return to their job in the foreseeable future, don't decide anything alone. The operations manager takes advice and considers reasonable accommodation (changes to the job, hours or role) under equality law before any other option.

## Pay and leave

- Statutory sick pay runs for a limited number of days a year set by law. Company sick pay, if any, is **Set by LeisureWorld**. Make sure they know when pay will change.
- Annual leave keeps building up during certified sick leave. Leave they couldn't take because of illness carries over for the time the law allows. Ask the operations manager.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- The absence and its extensions in **Rota: Absences**.
- Contact, the referral, consent and the return plan as notes on their **HR** record, without medical details. The occupational health report itself is kept by the operations manager (HR-SOP-09).
