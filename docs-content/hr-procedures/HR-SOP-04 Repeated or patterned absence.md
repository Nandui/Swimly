# [HR-SOP-04] Repeated or patterned absence

Tags: SOP, Draft

**Next review:** October 2027

## Purpose

What to do when someone's absence reaches a trigger point, or follows a pattern, so that it is handled early, fairly and the same way for everyone.

Applies to: line managers and centre managers, at every LeisureWorld centre.

## Trigger points

A review starts when, in any rolling 12 months, someone has:

- **Set by LeisureWorld** separate absences, or
- **Set by LeisureWorld** days off in total, or
- a pattern, for example often off on a Monday or Friday, next to a day off or a bank holiday, around school holidays, or on a shift they asked to swap and couldn't.

Turnfin shows each person's absences in the last 12 months on their HR record. Absences answered **Is it the same thing again?** with yes count as one.

## What never counts towards a trigger

- Absence related to pregnancy or maternity.
- Absence for a disability, where it would be fair to discount it. Speak to the operations manager first.
- Absence because of an injury at work recorded in Incidents.
- Force majeure, compassionate, parental, carer's, domestic violence and other statutory leave (HR-SOP-08).

## Step 1: informal talk

1. When a trigger is reached, the line manager holds a private, informal talk within **Set by LeisureWorld** days.
2. Show them the dates. Ask whether there is an underlying reason, at work or outside it, and whether anything would help.
3. Agree what happens next: support (for example a change of shift, occupational health, the employee assistance programme, **Set by LeisureWorld**), and that attendance will be looked at again in **Set by LeisureWorld** months.
4. Write a short note on their HR record, visibility **Shared with them**, saying what was discussed and agreed. No medical details.

## Step 2: formal stages

Only if absence carries on reaching triggers after the informal talk and the review period.

1. Speak to the centre manager or operations manager before starting.
2. Invite them in writing to a formal attendance meeting, with at least **Set by LeisureWorld** days' notice. Say they may bring a colleague or a trade union representative.
3. At the meeting, go through the record, listen to their side, and consider any medical advice.
4. Decide the outcome and confirm it in writing, with the review period and their right to appeal to **Set by LeisureWorld**.
5. The stages are **Set by LeisureWorld (for example first written warning, final written warning, then possible dismissal)**. Never skip a stage without advice.

Follow LeisureWorld's disciplinary procedure and the Workplace Relations Commission code of practice on grievance and disciplinary procedures: tell them the concern, let them answer, let them be accompanied, decide fairly, and let them appeal.

## What gets recorded in Turnfin

- The absences, in **Rota: Absences**.
- The informal talk and each formal stage as notes on their **HR** record. Letters go in their personnel file (**Set by LeisureWorld: where**).
