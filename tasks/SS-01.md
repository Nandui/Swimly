## Problem
The search field, a filled 'Search' button and the segmented filter sit on the canvas above the list. A floating '1–5 of 5 swimmers' and 'Surname A–Z' line sits between them, and pagination is hand-rolled outside the panel ('1 / 5' with arrows). The list is padded twice: .directory draws a panel and poolside turns the item-group into a second one, so rows sit 32px in and phones lose 64px. Captions are 14px; 'No member number ·' wraps and orphans the separator, and that filler repeats on every row. Rows have no avatar or site column. The header is hand-rolled, with actions between the H1 and the description at 375. Segment counts ignore the search ('Active 5' next to '0 swimmers'). The empty state is a raw Empty with dead classes and two buttons for the same action, and the first-row radius rule is dead.

## Change (original)
Use PageHeader (title 'Swimmers', one description, actions 'Parent accounts' (outline) then 'Add swimmer' (primary)). Put SearchField (label 'Find a swimmer', Enter submits, no Search button), SegmentedLinks and the list inside one .pc-panel. Delete the .directory panel declarations, the .directory [data-slot=item-group] rule, the row border, radius and padding duplicates, and the dead first-row radius (student-directory.module.css:3-11, 12-28, 115-117), so poolside draws one panel. Rows: 40px initials Avatar, name at body/600, one .pc-row-hint caption joined with ' · ' (omit the member number when it is absent; no 'No member number'), a Site column, and the status Tag with its icon. Move the count into LinkPagination ('1 to 25 of 1,102') at the panel foot and drop 'Surname A–Z'. Pass filters.q to getStudentCounts. Use EmptyState with one action ('Clear filters'). In these files, no tracking-*, leading-*, text-base or font-medium.
