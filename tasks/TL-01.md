## Problem
Home timeline: a 30-minute class gets one 56px column and truncates to 'Otte…' at 1024 and 768. The grid pads to four hours, starts at the first session (so there is no now line before it), has no lane icon, says 'Area', and uses icon-only block tags that need a separate Key row. The phone title stays 'Classes today' over an 'On now and next' list, and track sizes are inline literals. Rota Day and Today each hand-roll a timeline: bordered panels, sunken header bands of floating hour labels that overlap at 768 ('06:0008:00') and clip '22:00' at 1280, and ghost-Button shift bars (44px in 40px lanes, so blocks spill over dividers and are cut off; blue text on red or amber tints; 999px stadiums). 30-minute classes show 'O.'. Teaching marks are 27x8px links. Day plan's 'Add activity' and late shifts sit hidden in a horizontal scroller at 1024 and below. Activities cycle decorative TONES (two identical). Group headings are blue (decoration) and Today's are plain divs. The now line cuts through labels on Day and hides under blocks on Today. Legend swatches are 1.02-1.27:1. Helpers (span, Lane, booking bar, dayRange) are duplicated.
