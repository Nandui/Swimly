## Problem
Help has no rounded frame (a bare max-w-6xl column with 88px gutters). It adds a ThemeFlip button the HPIndex mockup does not have, uses ArrowLeft, and has a footer panel whose LifeBuoy icon stacks above the text (.pc-panel's unlayered column beats flex-row), with font-medium and leading-relaxed. Its skip link is a 24px unpadded word. At 375 the tools wrap onto their own row. `.pc-panel` plus `hidden md:flex` or `hidden lg:flex` fails because the unlayered display wins: on phones the desktop topic list shows as well as the collapsible, and on an article at 375 the contents rail squeezes the text into about 120px, one word per line. Index: topics are blue ghost buttons that wrap their labels; common tasks are underlined links; each guide is a three-line Item (a 4,928px page); the search is 52px with a hidden label; panel headings mix 14px and 18px. Article: header, meta and tools inside the panel; an ad-hoc back pair; 'Before you start' in an opacity-derived box; outlined step circles; underlined related links; mixed outline and ghost tools; capped at max-w-3xl.

(1) The shared `.skip-link` is not available on /help. It is defined only in docs.css as `:where(.turnfin-docs) .skip-link`, and the (help) group never loads docs.css. A live probe of /help found 0 skip-link rules: a test `a.skip-link` rendered position static, 18px tall, always visible. On /refunds the same class gives a 44px box with a 6px radius, so it would not pass the "44px pill" acceptance even where it is styled.

(2) Tailwind utilities on `.pc-row` lose to the unlayered rule in poolside.css:528. Probed live: `a.pc-row.min-h-11.bg-ui-muted.py-2` still computes to min-height 64px, white background and 12px padding. Also, there is no `pc-row[aria-current]` rule (no "current-row soft fill"), no compact row and no sunken row anywhere in poolside.css. The HPIndex/HPArticle mockups show topic and contents rows at 44px with 8px 16px padding, a --psoft fill for the current row, and sunken step rows with a filled 24px primary number.

(3) Wrapping the sticky "On this page" nav in `<div className='hidden lg:block'>` stops it sticking. The nav's containing block becomes the wrapper, which is exactly as tall as the nav.

(4) Removing h-13 without moving the search icon (`top-4`) and the clear button (`top-1 size-11`) leaves the icon 4px low and pushes the clear button 4px out of the 44px input.
