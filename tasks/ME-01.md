## Problem
Me's copied tokens drift from Work: type 13/15/18/24 with no 28px H1; H1 at 700 with tracking; 500 labels with a 500 font import; literal 18px, 13px, 12px and 28px sizes; and --pc-radius-inner at 16px where Work uses 999px. Tokens the shared parts need are missing (--pc-radius-card, --pc-yellow, --pc-on-yellow, --pc-hover, --pc-text-figure, --pc-control-pad). Lists are hairline-divided rows with no radius, inset or v2 hover, and the title underlines on hover. Rows wrap badly: chevrons drop under tags, shift rows squeeze to one word per line, and a second tag runs past the panel. The tab bar is a flat strip on phones and a 236px strip of touching labels at 768px and up (pinned top:65px); the current tab is text only; labels 12/500; icons 22px; More uses 'Menu'; no scroll padding. The top bar is a sticky bordered strip with a 16px visible fin, 'Me' in decorative blue at 13px, and a blue ghost Sign out; sign-in scales the brand with an inline fontSize 22. Component geometry is off (tags 26px, chevrons 18px, checkboxes 22px). Wide screens stretch block buttons to 640px and detach the top bar from the column.
