## Problem
Today's classes are a divided Item list with plain '16:00 / 16:30' (no 'to'), four stacked metadata lines, an icon-less 'Later' tag, and no block state for running, next or cover. 'Quick view' is a grey secondary button next to an outline 'Cancel session', both without icons. The header is hand-rolled, with two lines ('4 Oct 2026 · site' and 'Today's classes, at a glance.'). The filter tabs become a 2x2 grid inside a 999px stadium at 375 and show counts at full weight. Rows read '1 swimmers'. Refresh is outline here but ghost on Schedule. Phase labels ('Later', 'On now', 'Upcoming') differ from home. Notes in the dialogs are ad-hoc boxes, and the 'Billing follow-up' link does not name its destination.

## Change (original)
1) PageHeader: title 'Duty manager', one description 'formatDay(today) · <site> · Today's classes, at a glance', actions outline 'Refresh' (RefreshCw) then 'Cancelled classes' (the link named after its destination). 2) Each class is a .pc-row: a .pc-block chip (data-state from the phase or cover: now, next, cover, done, off) with formatTimeRange as the time; title plus one .pc-row-hint 'Programme · Instructor · Pool · plural(n, swimmer)'; a status Tag from HOME_SESSION_META (On now, Coming up, Finished, Cancelled, plus a Cover needed entry), so duty, schedule and home share one map; actions outline 'Quick view' (Eye) and 'Cancel session' (CalendarX2), which become 44px icon buttons with aria-labels under 768px. 3) Filters: the TabsList default variant without 'grid w-full grid-cols-2', wrapping as pills, counts in .pc-seg-count. 4) cancel-session and billing-review notes become .pc-note. No tracking-tight or text-base.
