## Problem
The week picker is seven outlined 64px tiles on the canvas, plus a separate '28 Sept – 4 Oct' row with prev, Today and next. The summary is plain text, and the legend takes its own row. Cells are outlined white cards with cramped 1.3 line height, a lone availability icon and a floating 'Uncapped'; cover is the text '· Cover'; next, cover and full states have no block colour; the header reads 'Level / time'. In running cells the secondary text keeps the muted colour on the green block (4.15:1 light, 3.21:1 dark). With one start time the column stretches to about 900px. Badge labels are typed at the call site ('Now', 'Next', a grey 'Assessment') instead of coming from the map. Prev and next are ghost here but outline on Rota. calendar.module.css:2-55 is a dead first half, with letter-spacing (-.025em, .01em), literal line heights, a hover shadow and its own .sr-only. The header is hand-rolled; dates use another formatter; the tooltip says '1 enrolled swimmers'; the day pill's accessible name does not start with 'Today'.

## Change (original)
1) PageHeader (title 'Schedule', description 'formatDay · <site>', actions outline 'Refresh' with RefreshCw and primary 'Jump to now' with Clock). 2) One .pc-panel holding day navigation (outline round prev and next icon buttons around .pc-seg-item day pills, aria-pressed kept, with Today as a pill), the Booking sheet / Agenda SegmentedChoice, summary Tags with icons, and the assessment notice as Notice tone info. Delete the range row and .sheet-legend. 3) Cells are .pc-block with data-state (now, next, cover, done, off; full shown as done) and a .pc-block-tag availability ('2 free' or 'Full', with an icon), using 'No limit' wording. The header reads 'Level and time'. Time columns get a fixed width via colgroup (about 144px) with width:max-content on the table. 4) Phase labels, tones and icons come from one map: drop CALENDAR_PHASE_META's labels and point at HOME_SESSION_META (add an 'assessment' entry, purple, ClipboardCheck). 5) calendar.module.css: delete lines 2-55, letter-spacing, the hover box-shadow and the local .sr-only; use --pc-leading-* tokens. 6) Copy: formatTimeRange and formatDateRange; plural on 'enrolled swimmer'; the day pill name starts with its visible text ('Today, Sunday 4 October').
