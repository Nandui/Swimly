## Problem

Reorder chevrons sit at different x positions per row and look like expand toggles, and Edit and Archive are blue ghost icons (also on /programmes). Together is capped at 960px and /programmes/[id] at 1152px by pageWidthFor, so right edges do not line up. 'Start again' and 'add all 3' are inline links under 24px; chips are 16px boxes; the pager uses filled primary buttons; the picker shows 'Search swimmers…' but is named 'Add a child'; the copy uses em dashes. Cancelled classes: a hand-rolled two-line header; billing rows with border-y dividers, an h2 inside each row and no icon in the empty state; plain date text where SSCancellations shows a hatched date tile; the review dialog uses a divided list and ad-hoc boxes; pagination is hand-rolled. The 'Copy to another club' copy and the programme hint are in Title Case.
