## Problem

Rows put the tag inline after the name, wrap it under long names, use body-size second lines plus a third caption line (100-130px tall), and end in ArrowRight. They have no avatars or tiles. At 375 the sign-off and expiring rows squeeze their text to one word per line beside the buttons. Name links are 20px targets. Filters and results sit in two panels with a count floating between them, and the 'Show' select duplicates the tiles. Tiles copy refunds.css with no open state. Panel titles touch their lists (0px). Headers are hand-built .module-heading. View switches are underlined links, and Courses changes its H1 to 'Retired courses'. The primary action comes first (Sign off, then Not yet; Record it, then Decline). Row actions mix outline and blue ghost. Retire is a field-less FormDialog with a blue confirm; the Cancel training dialog offers 'Cancel' next to 'Cancel training'. The Assign training chooser has six 'Everyone on…' outline buttons and a nested scroll. Choice labels are 600. The course switch is unlabelled. No bar item is current on a training record, and there is no back link or site picker. Empty states use .module-empty with wrong guidance on the retired view.
