## Problem

The status filter mixes a grey secondary button with blue ghosts. The 'Find account' button sits about 28px below the field. The result is a ruled section, and pagination is hand-rolled ('Page 1 of N'). Parent changes: a second intro paragraph with an underlined 'Show decided requests' link; a p-5 Card; actions on the left with the primary first; proposed values not emphasised; at 375 the key 'Parent proposes' column is cut off and hidden in a sideways scroll. The copy hard-codes the customer ('LeisureWorld Aquatics', 'Families from both sites', 'across both sites', 'midnight in Ireland'). Notes are ad-hoc rounded-ui-md boxes. The decided view's hint says decided requests 'appear here for review', and empty titles end in full stops.
