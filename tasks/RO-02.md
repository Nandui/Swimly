## Problem
The 'why the change?' fieldset in every started-week dialog uses a 999px radius on a 401px box, drawing an arc through the radios. Choice labels make hints heavier than titles. 'Cancel duty' and 'Cancel booking' ask for danger text but render blue ghosts. 'Duty' and 'shift' name the same thing in one view. Absences: section titles on the canvas; rows without avatars; 14px ink second lines; at 375 the Return to work row squeezes to four lines and an orphan blue trash icon; 'Nobody is off' contradicts the week plan for maya; copy about a retired roster upload. Bookings: 'New booking' is outline and left-aligned on phones; rows with an outline 'Plan who' (no icon) and blue ghost 'Cancel booking'; nested brackets in the qualification text; the weekend default dates give 'No sessions yet'. Today: explanation lines in 14px body; generic 'Give cover' buttons whose names differ; main content inside <aside>; Timepoint and Covered states as coloured text. Week plan and Day plan open the same editor in two containers (Sheet with a manual 'Saving…' versus FormDialog). The segment preview is an empty grey pill, clipped ('Pa', 'Unpa'), with no visible labels at 375. Day notes sit in a max-w-2xl panel titled by a field label, with Save appearing only after typing. Empty states use .module-empty.
