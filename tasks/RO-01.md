## Problem
Rota pages use the old .module-heading. On Week plan the actions drop to a left-aligned second row even at 1280. The H1 bolts on the site in a second weight ('Week plan: LeisureWorld…' at 400 grey). The week state is a call-site-coloured chip without an icon, shown to viewers who cannot change anything. A 'Bookings' button repeats the page bar. The same select plus 'Show' form is copied into four pages, truncating site names, while the mockups put the site in the tools. The week range is a bordered span that looks like a control. The sheet is a bordered table with gridlines, a sunken header band, a yellow-washed 'To fill' row (olive in dark) and a blue today column. Cells are ghost Buttons with blue text (blue on red, blue on amber) and 12px or pill corners, with text-[13px], and at 1280 every 'To fill' label is truncated ('Swim teac…', 'Off · find co…'). Day headers wrap '· day ›'. Empty days show 40% '+' marks (1.86:1). Tiles are bordered and tinted at the call site with 18px values, and 'Warnings' links nowhere. Hours show '0' for an absent person. Phones see only Mon and Tue in a sideways scroll. Accessible names do not match the visible text.

Day headers are .pc-timeline-hour-style tiles (data-now filled for today), with the whole tile linking to the Day plan (drop '· day ›').
