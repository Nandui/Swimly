# Turnfin logo

`turnfin.png` is the original, unmodified transparent PNG supplied by the owner
on 17 September 2026 for the Turnfin staff portal. Source: the attached
`turnfin.png` file. It is not generated artwork.

`turnfin-fin-mask.png` is made from it: the logo's silhouette (its alpha channel, cropped to the
logo and squared), redrawn at 2560px with a crisp edge. It is the mask for the fin on the ground
(DESIGN.md, The fin); white with alpha, no colour of its own.
