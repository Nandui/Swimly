# What's new in Turnfin

Patch notes for everyone who uses Turnfin at the swim school, the desk and the office. Newest first. You can read them in Turnfin too: open your picture at the top right and choose **What's new**.

**How versions are numbered:** the middle number goes up when something new arrives (a module, a screen, a new way of working), and the last number when something is fixed or tidied without changing how you work. This list starts on 3 October 2026, when Turnfin moved to its new look; earlier changes are not listed.

## 1.60.0 · 21 October 2026 · Enrolments and moves done as development work

- Enrolments, moves between classes or centres and unenrolments done by Fernando Serina's account were mostly set-up work run for the developers, not real desk work. They now show as **System - scripted action done via development** in **Activity**, in a swimmer's **History** and in the Together records, instead of his name.
- They are left out of **Swim school analytics**: they no longer count as new swimmers, leavers or moves, or as anyone's work on the **Reception** dashboard. The swimmers themselves still count in classes, places and "% full" while they hold the place.
- This covers what his account did before today as well as from now on. Nothing has been deleted, and the log still knows which account did it.
- A superadmin can do the same for any account: open the person on **Staff** and choose **Mark place changes as system**. **Count their place changes again** stops it for new changes; earlier ones stay marked.
- While an account is marked, the confirmation before an enrolment or a move says that it will be recorded as system.

## 1.59.0 · 21 October 2026 · Rota: place "any time" sub-activities on the Plan

- A sub-activity done **any time** while someone is on an activity, such as **Answer the phone** on Reception, is now placed straight on the rota **Plan**, at the moment you choose.
- Open someone's day on the Plan: under each activity, **Place on their time** lists these sub-activities. Tap one, tap a time (or type one) inside their time on the activity, and choose **Place it**. You can place the same one more than once, such as the phone at 12:30 and again at 15:00.
- Once placed, it shows at its time on their day, on the phone view and in **Who's where**, which now lists the sub-activities in the half hour shown. Tap a placed one to move it or take it off.
- In **Admin, Activities**, these now read **Any time: placed on the Plan**, and they no longer come along by themselves when someone is put on the activity. Sub-activities at the start, at the end or at set times work as before.

## 1.58.0 · 21 October 2026 · Rota: areas ask for a role, not a certificate

- On the rota **Plan**, an area's own staff now show by the role it needs, such as **Lifeguard on duty** in the main pool, instead of the name of the certificate.
- The certificate still decides who can fill the place, and still shows in small print, so anyone without it gets the same warning as before.
- In **Admin, Areas**, each line in **Staff needed** now has a **Role** (from Admin, Positions) and **Who can fill it**. Choosing a role fills in the qualification it needs; change it only if that area needs something else. Areas you set up before already show the role that needs their certificate, so nothing needs entering again. Open them once to check the role is right.
- Lines that ask for people on an activity, such as **at least 1 on Serving customers**, stay as they are and show next to the roles.

## 1.57.0 · 21 October 2026 · Swimming courses in the parents app

- Swimming courses can now be booked in the parents app. When you put on or change a **Pool** course, tick **Show in the parents app** and it shows there until its first session.
- Courses for 18 and over are for adults, who sign up in the app with their own account and book a place for themselves. Their first booking adds them to the swimmer records. Other courses show to parents, who book a place for one of their children if the child is old enough.
- A place booked in the app is held, not paid. It shows on the course and on the **To phone** list, already linked to the swimmer, with a reference and the 72 hours you have to phone them and take payment.
- When a course is full, the app says so and gives the centre's phone number. There is no waiting list in the app.
- Each site now has a **Reception phone** in **Admin > Sites**. Fill it in so the app can show it.

## 1.56.1 · 21 October 2026 · Places at each level show how full each level is

- In **Analytics**, **Places at each level** now gives every level a bar the same length, so you can compare them at a glance. The blue part is how much of that level is filled and the grey part is what is free.
- Each level shows how full it is as a percentage at the end of its row. Anyone waiting is shown beside the figures with an orange dot.
- When you open a slot in **When classes are full**, its levels use the same bars and percentages.

## 1.56.0 · 21 October 2026 · A clear check before every move and enrolment

- Whenever you move or enrol a swimmer, Turnfin now shows one clear picture of what is about to happen before anything changes: the swimmer's name in large type, the class they leave (it ends today) and the class they join (it starts today), each with its day, time, level and centre.
- When the move takes a swimmer from one centre to another, a **Transfer between centres** banner shows both centres in large type, so a change of centre cannot be missed.
- The confirm button waits five seconds before you can press it ("Confirm move in 5", then "Confirm move"). If you go back and change something, the wait starts again. **Cancel** and **Back** always work straight away.
- This covers enrolling from a class page, from a swimmer's profile and from **Awaiting enrolment**, enrolling from a waitlist, moving a swimmer to another class (including from **Awaiting moves**), and **Move them here** on **Together**.
- The check also lists anything worth knowing: a level change, a level they haven't finished yet, a child assessed at another centre, and the Legend agreement.

## 1.55.0 · 21 October 2026 · Short courses book people from the swimmer records

- **Book someone on** (short courses) and **Add a candidate** (Academy courses) now start with **Find the swimmer**. Search by name, member number, date of birth or a parent's name, and pick the right person. Their member number, contacts, emergency contact and medical notes come with them.
- If they are not on the swimmer records yet, choose **Add them as a new swimmer**, fill in their details, and they are added and booked on in one go. Turnfin tells you if someone with the same name and date of birth is already there, so nobody is added twice. Adults on gym and lifeguard courses get a swimmer record too; staff on Academy courses stay as staff.
- Each person on a course shows their **member number**, and a red **Medical notes** tag when there are any. Open the pencil to read their full record, with **Open their profile** if you can see Swimmers. Change their details on the swimmer record, and every course they are on follows.
- The register shows each person's member number too.
- People booked before today show **No swimmer record** and a **Link to swimmer** button, so you can match them up when you have a moment. Nothing else about them changes.
- When you log a call as **Paid** for an Academy place held online, Turnfin asks you to find them on the swimmer records or add them first. Their details from the booking are filled in for you.
- A swimmer's profile now lists the short courses and Academy courses they have been on, under **Details**, in **Other courses**.

## 1.53.0 · 21 October 2026 · Areas need people on an activity, and sub-activities at several times

- In **Admin > Areas**, an area's **Staff needed** can now ask for people on an activity: for example, Reception needs **at least 1 on Serving customers**, from 09:00 to 21:00. Leave the times empty for whenever the area is open. Add as many lines as you need, such as a second one for 2 people at the busy time.
- On the rota **Plan**, only people actually on that activity in the area (or in one of its parts, such as Till 1) count. If nobody is on it for a stretch, it shows under **Not covered yet** with the time and the reason, such as "Nobody on it yet".
- **Choose someone** for one of these puts the person on that activity in the area, so their day says what they are doing there and the activity's usual sub-activities come with it.
- Your existing lines, such as "2 lifeguards while open", work as before.
- **Sub-activities** can now happen at several set times: for example, a cash drop at 12:00 and 16:00. Set the times in **Admin > Activities > Sub-activities**, and add or remove times for one person's day from their day on the Plan.
## 1.52.0 · 21 October 2026 · Coinage: ask accounts for change for the tills

- **Coinage** is new, beside Refunds under Front of house. The desk asks accounts for coins for a till, and accounts decides and records the order, the same way refund requests go to finance.
- **Request coinage** asks for the site, the till or area it is for, the day it is needed by, and how much of each coin in euro, from €2 down to 1c (for example €50 of €2 coins). Turnfin shows how many coins that is and adds up the total. Save a draft or send it to accounts.
- Accounts gets an email when a request arrives, and can take it on, approve it, decline it or ask the desk for more information. Nobody decides their own request.
- Once ordered, accounts records the day it was ordered, the delivery day and the order reference. The desk gets an email at each step and can follow every request, with its full history, under **Coinage requests**.
- Home shows accounts how many requests wait to be decided and ordered, and the desk a **Request coinage** button.
- Coinage stays hidden until an administrator gives roles a level in **Admin > Roles**: **Use** for the desks, **Manage** for accounts.
## 1.51.0 · 21 October 2026 · Together: move them from there

- Each option on **Together** now has a **Move them here** button (or **Move Robin here** when only one child needs to change), for anyone who can enrol swimmers.
- Before anything changes, you see every child on one card: the class they leave (it ends today), the class they join (it starts today), and whether they change class, change level or get a new place. A child already in that class is marked "Stays as they are".
- If a child has more than one place, choose which one to move from, or keep it and add the new class too.
- The usual checks still apply. A level they haven't finished needs a reason, which goes on their record. A new place asks about the Legend agreement. You get a warning when the child was assessed at another centre. A class that has filled up stops the move.
- Everyone moves at once or nobody does, and each change goes into the activity log with your name. Attendance and marks from the old class stay in their history.

## 1.49.0 · 21 October 2026 · Together: try other levels

## 1.48.0 · 20 October 2026 · Rota: a new Plan, the day's diary

- **Plan** is new, built from the start as the day's diary: open a day and anyone can see who is where, when they change, their breaks and what still needs someone. Everything already planned is still there.
- It opens on your own department and on today. Pick another department, or **Every department**, and any day of the week from the bar under the title. Each day says how many things are still to fill, or **Done**.
- **Not covered yet** comes first: everything that needs people and has nobody, or too few, soonest first, with the reason ("Nobody yet", "1 more needed", "Riley is off", "Liam is on a break"). An area's own lifeguards and the activities in it add up: someone teaching aqua-fit is not one of the pool's lifeguards then.
- **Choose someone** shows the best fits first: free then, able to do it and from your department, then people from other departments you can borrow straight away, then who can't and why. Each says what it does to their day, such as "On shift then" or "Their day becomes 07:00–16:00", and their hours this week. You can give part of the time.
- **Who's where** shows, at any time of the day (now, on today), each area and activity with who is on it, anything short of people, who is on a break and the next change.
- **The day, person by person** has one line for each person: covering an area is blue, activities and classes green, bookings violet, breaks dashed, time not given yet dotted, and what is not covered sits in red at the top. On a phone each person's day is a short list.
- Choose a person to see their day from start to finish, with what to look at (breaks still to place, a qualification, two things at once). From there change their times, take them off something, give their free time to something not covered, place their breaks and change their shifts.
- **Areas** (Admin) can now belong to a department and have parts of their own: the 25 m and 18 m as part of Pool, or Till 1 and Till 2 as part of Front desk. An area's needs show on its department's Plan, and **Who's where** lists people under each area and its parts.
- **Activities** (Admin) can have **sub-activities**: the smaller jobs done by whoever is on it, such as open the cash till at the start, a cash drop at 12:00, answer the phone any time, and close the cash till at the end. The usual ones come with the activity when you put someone on it.
- A person's day lists their sub-activities under each thing they are on, with their times. **Sub-activities** there ticks or unticks any of them for that day and changes a set time. They never need anyone extra.
- **Add someone** brings a person into the day before anything is given to them, and **Add to the day** adds something that needs people. Copy, Export week and Share with staff work as before.
- **Bookings** has **Staff each kind needs**: how many staff a party, school lessons, lane hire and so on need. A new booking starts with that number, and 0 means it needs nobody, so lane hire stays off **Not covered yet**.
- **Today** opens with five figures: gaps left, who is on now, what starts in the next three hours, who is off and changes to update in Timepoint.
- **Bookings** opens with three figures, splits running bookings from finished ones and shows how many places on each booking are filled.

## 1.47.0 · 20 October 2026 · Site Monitoring: readings out of range go to managers

- Close an action by saying what was done. Closed actions stay listed with who closed them and what they wrote, and can be opened again.
- Your home page shows how many are waiting. Site Monitoring is hidden until your role is given a level for it.
## 1.46.0 · 20 October 2026 · Managers: a CCTV log

Each time someone checks CCTV footage, they record it: who checked it and when, anyone else watching, why it was checked, what they were looking for, which cameras and times they watched, what they saw, and any copy saved or handed over.
- You can add the incident report's number, such as INC-00042, so the record links to the report.
- A saved record can't be changed or deleted. If something comes to light later, press **Add a note** and it shows under the record with your name and the time.
- **Record** lets people record viewings and read their own. **Review** also lets them read every viewing at their sites and add notes. Nobody sees the CCTV log until an administrator gives their role a level under **Admin, Roles**.

## 1.43.0 · 20 October 2026 · Training: turn an SOP into a course with AI

- In **Training, Courses**, people who build courses now have **Draft from a document**. Give it an SOP, a policy or a handbook section (a PDF, a Word document, a text file or a photo of the pages), or paste the text, and the AI writes a course from it: lessons, flashcards, checklists, practice questions and a final exam.
- The new course is never live straight away. It opens in the builder unpublished, with a comment listing what to check, such as gaps or unclear steps in the document. Read it, fix anything, then publish it as usual.
- Names, phone numbers and emails in the document are swapped for the role, such as "the duty manager". Leave out anything personal before you send a document.

## 1.41.0 · 20 October 2026 · Add a swimmer to Awaiting enrolment yourself

## 1.37.0 · 19 October 2026 · Incidents: ask for CCTV footage by email

- On an incident report, **CCTV footage > Ask for footage** sends a request to whoever has the recording: someone on the team, or anyone by email, such as the security company.
- It fills in the day, the time and the place from the report. Add which cameras you need. They see only what you write there, never who was involved or what happened.
- They get an email with a link to upload the videos straight to the report, without signing in. The link works for 14 days. Videos can be up to 5 GB each, as the recorder exports them.
- You get an email when a video arrives. It shows under **CCTV footage** on the report, where you can play or download it. Only people who triage reports at that site can see it.
- **Send again** sends a new link if the first one expired or got lost. **Stop the link** closes it once you have what you need.
- Every request and every video is in the report's history and listed in the full record.

## 1.36.0 · 19 October 2026 · Swim school analytics: a new dashboard look

## 1.35.0 · 19 October 2026 · Incidents: triage that fits each kind of report

- **Triage** on an incident report now follows what the report is about. A theft is rated by what was lost, a fight by how threatening it was, a missing child by how long they were missing, a pool contamination by how long the pool was out, and so on. The same five levels still decide whether it can be closed at triage or needs investigating and a manager's sign-off.
- Under the two ratings you can see what each level means for that kind of report.
- Each kind asks the few things worth recording: for a theft, whose property it was, roughly its worth, whether the Gardaí were told and their incident number, and whether CCTV was saved. Behaviour, missing children, chemicals, pool water, equipment faults and fires each have their own. None of them is required.
- When a staff member is named on a report, or abuse was aimed at one, triage asks what support they were offered.
- The Health and Safety Authority questions that fit come first: the injury ones only when someone was hurt. The others are still there under **Check the HSA questions anyway**.
- A missing child report always asks who told the parent or guardian.
- The answers show under **Triage** on the report and in the full record.
